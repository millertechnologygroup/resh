Handle: system
Verb: env.list
Definition: Lists the environment variables of the resh process or of another
  process by PID, masking sensitive values by default.

Usage:
  resh system://.env.list [pid=<n>] [names=<list>]
    [prefix_filters=<list>] [regex_filters=<list>]
    [include_values=true|false] [include_sensitive=true|false]
    [truncate_length=<n>] [max_vars=<n>] [sort_by=name|length|none]
    [sort_order=asc|desc] [include_raw=true|false]
    [include_paths=true|false] [format=json|text] [input=<json>]
    defaults: pid is resh itself, include_values=true,
      include_sensitive=false, truncate_length=512 (0 = no limit),
      max_vars=1024, sort_by=name, sort_order=asc, include_raw and
      include_paths false, format=json

Example:
  resh --json-pretty system://.env.list names=HOME,SHELL
