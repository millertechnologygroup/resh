Handle: fs
Verb: quota
Definition: Reports the disk space and inode quota usage and limits of one user,
  group, or project, or of every subject, on the filesystem holding a path,
  using quota or repquota.

Usage:
  resh fs://<alias>.quota [path=<path>] [subject=<name-or-id>]
    [subject_type=auto|user|group|project] [all_subjects=true|false]
    [units=auto|blocks|bytes] [include_space=true|false]
    [include_inodes=true|false] [include_grace=true|false]
    [resolve_uid_gid=true|false] [timeout_ms=<ms>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty fs://local.quota path=/home subject=alice

  Shown with --json-pretty. Without path the root filesystem is queried,
  and without subject the current user is queried. all_subjects=true
  lists every subject on the filesystem (repquota, needs root). Sizes are
  bytes, or 1 KiB blocks with units=blocks. Defaults: subject_type=auto,
  units=auto, the include switches and resolve_uid_gid true,
  timeout_ms=5000.
