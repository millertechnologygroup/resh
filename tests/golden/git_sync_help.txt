Handle: git
Verb: sync
Definition: Fetches from a remote, brings the current branch up to date by
  fast-forward or, when the histories have diverged, by the pull_strategy
  (rebase, merge, or ff_only), and then pushes it, pushing nothing when that
  fails.

Usage:
  resh git://<alias>.sync path=<repo-dir> [remote=<name>]
    [branch=<name>] [remote_branch=<name>]
    [pull_strategy=rebase|merge|ff_only] [push=true|false]
    [push_tags=true|false] [force_push=true|false]
    [allow_uncommitted=true|false] [stash_uncommitted=true|false]
    [abort_on_conflict=true|false] [author_name=<name>]
    [author_email=<email>] [dry_run=true|false]
    [known_hosts_path=<file>] [timeout_ms=<ms>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty git://main.sync path=/srv/app pull_strategy=rebase
