Handle: event
Verb: hooks.enable
Definition: Enables one of the built-in hooks fs, log, or mq for this and every
  later emit of the user.

Usage:
  resh event://.hooks.enable name=fs|log|mq

Example:
  resh event://.hooks.enable name=mq

Example Output:
  {"changed":true,"enabled":true,"hook":"mq"}

  changed is false if the hook was already enabled.
