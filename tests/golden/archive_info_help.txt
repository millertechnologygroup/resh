Handle: archive
Verb: info
Definition: Reports archive-level details such as format, compression, size,
  entry counts, compression ratio, and modification time range, as JSON.

Usage:
  resh archive://.info archive=<path> [scan_entries=true|false]
    [max_entries=<n>] [max_total_bytes=<n>] [format=<archive-format>]
    [compression=auto|none|gzip|xz|zstd]
    [fail_on_missing_archive=true|false] [timeout_ms=<n>]
    [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty archive://.info archive=logs.tar.gz
