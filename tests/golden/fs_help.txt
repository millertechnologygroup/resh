Handle: fs

Description:
  The fs handle manages mounted filesystems on the local host, covering mounting
  and unmounting, mount table listings and lockfile snapshots, disk usage,
  quotas, resizing, and consistency checks. It reads /proc/mounts and statvfs
  data and runs system tools such as mount, umount, quota, repquota, resize2fs,
  xfs_growfs, btrfs, and fsck. The URL host is a free-form alias, such as
  fs://local, that is echoed back in the JSON result.

Available Verbs:
  list-mounts     Lists the mounted filesystems from the mount table with their
                  options and read-only, pseudo, network, and loop flags, as
                  JSON.
  usage           Reports size, used, and free space and inode counts for each
                  mounted filesystem, or an aggregate total, as JSON.
  snapshot        Captures the current mount table, with usage and filesystem
                  metadata, as a deterministic fs-lock/v1 lockfile string.
  quota           Reports the disk space and inode quota usage and limits of one
                  user, group, or project, or of every subject, on the
                  filesystem holding a path, using quota or repquota.
  quota_summary   Aggregates the quota usage and limits of one user, group, or
                  project, or of every subject, across the mounted filesystems,
                  listing filesystems without quotas separately.
  mount           Mounts a device, network share, or bind source on a target
                  directory, creating the directory if needed, and reports the
                  result as JSON.
  unmount         Unmounts the filesystems matching a mount point or source
                  device, optionally with their child mounts, and reports the
                  result as JSON.
  umount          Alias for unmount.
  resize          Grows or shrinks a filesystem, located by mount point or
                  device, to an absolute size or by a delta using resize2fs,
                  xfs_growfs, or btrfs.
  check           Checks a filesystem for errors with fsck, xfs_repair, or
                  btrfs, read-only unless repair is allowed, and reports the
                  tool output and analysis as JSON.
  fsck            Alias for check.

Usage:
  resh fs://<alias>.<verb>[(key=value,...)]

For help with a specific verb:
  resh fs <verb> --help
