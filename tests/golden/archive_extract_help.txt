Handle: archive
Verb: extract
Definition: Extracts the entries of an archive into a destination directory,
  rejecting absolute paths and parent traversal by default, and reports a
  manifest as JSON.

Usage:
  resh archive://.extract archive=<path> destination=<dir>
    [includes=<json-array>] [excludes=<json-array>]
    [overwrite=true|false] [strip_components=<n>]
    [create_destination=true|false] [allow_absolute_paths=true|false]
    [allow_parent_traversal=true|false] [allow_symlinks=true|false]
    [follow_symlinks=false] [max_entries=<n>]
    [max_total_bytes=<n>] [max_file_bytes=<n>]
    [format=<archive-format>] [compression=auto|none|gzip|xz|zstd]
    [fail_on_missing_archive=true|false] [include_manifest=true|false]
    [timeout_ms=<n>] [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty archive://.extract archive=logs.tar.gz destination=restore
