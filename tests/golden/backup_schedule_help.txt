Handle: backup
Verb: schedule
Definition: Reports the backup command for a schedule, the cron handle command
  that installs it, and the detected scheduler, without installing a timer or
  cron job or writing any file.

Usage:
  resh backup://<profile>.schedule when=<cron> src=<path>[;<path>...]
    [backend=auto|restic|borg|rsync|tar] [repo_url=<repo>]
    [password_file=<path>] [enabled=true|false]
    [json_pretty=true|false]
    when: five crontab fields or an @ keyword

Example:
  resh backup://db.schedule when=@daily src=/etc backend=tar repo_url=/srv/b
