Handle: backup
Verb: schedule
Definition: Installs a scheduled create for the profile as a systemd timer or
  crontab line through the cron handle, or reports what would be installed when
  dry_run is true.

Usage:
  resh backup://<profile>.schedule when=<cron> src=<path>[;<path>...]
    [backend=auto|restic|borg|rsync|tar] [repo_url=<repo>]
    [password_file=<path>] [enabled=true|false]
    [scheduler=auto|cron|systemd] [dry_run=true|false]
    [timeout_ms=<ms>] [json_pretty=true|false]
    when: five crontab fields or an @ keyword (checked by cron add)

Example:
  resh backup://db.schedule when=@daily src=/e backend=tar repo_url=/b dry_run=1
