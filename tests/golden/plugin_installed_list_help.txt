Handle: plugin
Verb: installed.list
Definition: Lists the plugins in the local plugin store with their version,
  enabled state, and health, with filtering, sorting, and pagination.

Usage:
  resh plugin://installed.list [enabled=true|false]
    [name=<name>] [prefix=<text>] [tag=<tag>]
    [source=registry|url|file] [limit=<n>] [offset=<n>]
    [sort=name|installed_at|updated_at] [order=asc|desc]
    [format=full|summary|names]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty plugin://installed.list format=names
