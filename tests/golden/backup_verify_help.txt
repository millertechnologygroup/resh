Handle: backup
Verb: verify
Definition: Checks repository integrity with the backend's check command, or
  reads every tar archive or rsync snapshot, reading all data when mode is
  thorough or data.

Usage:
  resh backup://<profile>.verify
    [backend=auto|restic|borg|rsync|tar] [repo_url=<repo>]
    [password=<secret>|password_file=<path>]
    [mode=quick|thorough|data] [snapshot_id=<id>]
    [timeout_ms=<ms>] [json_pretty=true|false]
    snapshot_id: borg, tar and rsync only.
    timeout_ms default 3600000, for the whole verb

Example:
  resh --json-pretty backup://db.verify backend=restic repo_url=/srv/restic
