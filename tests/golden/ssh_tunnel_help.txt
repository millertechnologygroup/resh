Handle: ssh
Verb: tunnel
Definition: Runs a local, remote, or dynamic (SOCKS) port forward through the
  system ssh client until a limit, a timeout, or an interrupt ends it, and
  reports the traffic it carried.

Usage:
  resh ssh://user@host[:port].tunnel mode=local|remote|dynamic
    [local_bind_host=<addr>] [local_bind_port=<n>]
    [remote_bind_host=<addr>] [remote_bind_port=<n>]
    [remote_dest_host=<host>] [remote_dest_port=<n>]
    [local_dest_host=<host>] [local_dest_port=<n>]
    [socks_version=socks5|socks4] [allow_wildcard_binds=true|false]
    [tunnel_timeout_ms=<ms>] [idle_timeout_ms=<ms>]
    [max_connections=<n>] [max_bytes_in=<n>] [max_bytes_out=<n>]
    [host=<host>] [port=<n>] [username=<name>]
    [auth_method=auto|key|agent|password] [password=<pw>]
    [identity_path=<file>] [identity_data=<key>] [identity_passphrase=<pp>]
    [agent_socket=<path>] [known_hosts_mode=strict|accept-new|insecure]
    [known_hosts_path=<file>] [connect_timeout_ms=<ms>] [timeout_ms=<ms>]
    [dry_run=true|false] [format=json|text]

Example:
  resh ssh://alice@bastion.tunnel mode=dynamic local_bind_port=1080 dry_run=true

  max_connections, max_bytes_in, max_bytes_out, or interrupted).
