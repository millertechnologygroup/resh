Handle: firewall
Verb: rules.list
Definition: Lists the rules of one backend as JSON, optionally filtered by
  table, chain, direction, action, protocol, port, address, interface, or
  comment text.

Usage:
  resh firewall://.rules.list
    [backend=auto|iptables|nftables|ufw|firewalld]
    [family=any|ipv4|ipv6] [table=<table>] [chain=<chain>]
    [direction=<direction>] [action=<action>] [proto=<proto>]
    [sport=<port>] [dport=<port>] [saddr=<cidr>] [daddr=<cidr>]
    [in_iface=<iface>] [out_iface=<iface>] [comment_contains=<text>]
    [include_backend_raw=true|false] [include_counters=true|false]
    [max_rules=<n>] [timeout_ms=<ms>] [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty firewall://.rules.list family=ipv4 dport=22
