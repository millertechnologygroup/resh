Handle: cron
Verb: rm
Definition: Removes matching jobs from crontabs and deletes matching systemd
  timers, by default along with their unit files.

Usage:
  resh cron://<label>.rm [id=<id>] [schedule=<expr>] [command=<cmd>]
    [match_command=<text>] [match_comment=<text>] [unit_name=<name>]
    [match_unit=<text>] [backend=both|cron|systemd]
    [scope=all|current|user|system] [users=<user,...>]
    [cron_file=<path>] [unit_scope=auto|user|system|both]
    [remove_units=true|false] [require_match=true|false]
    [dry_run=true|false] [format=json|text] [timeout_ms=<ms>]
    every selector given must match; cron_file: /etc/crontab or
    /etc/cron.d/<name> only, and replaces scope

Example:
  resh cron://local.rm id=backup backend=cron scope=current dry_run=true

Example Output:

    "backend": "cron",
    "cron_modified_sources": [
      "/var/spool/cron/alice"

  nothing is changed; at least one selector is required.
