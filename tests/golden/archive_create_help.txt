Handle: archive
Verb: create
Definition: Creates an unencrypted tar, tar.gz, tar.xz, tar.zst, zip, or gzip
  archive from source files and directories, with optional glob filters, and
  reports the archived files as JSON (password= is refused).

Usage:
  resh archive://.create output=<path> sources=<json-array>
    [format=auto|tar|tar.gz|tar.xz|tar.zst|zip|gzip] [base_dir=<dir>]
    [compression_level=<n>] [include_patterns=<json-array>]
    [exclude_patterns=<json-array>] [include_hidden=true|false]
    [follow_symlinks=true|false] [overwrite=true|false]
    [preserve_permissions=true|false] [preserve_timestamps=true|false]
    [max_files=<n>] [max_size_mb=<n>] [progress=true|false]
    [timeout_ms=<n>] [format_output=json|text] [password=<refused>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty archive://.create output=logs.tar.gz 'sources=["app.log"]'
