Handle: firewall
Verb: rules.delete
Definition: Deletes the rule given by rule_id or every rule that matches the
  given filters, or only reports them and the backend commands when dry_run=true
  is given.

Usage:
  resh firewall://.rules.delete
    [backend=auto|iptables|nftables|ufw|firewalld]
    [family=any|ipv4|ipv6] [rule_id=<id>] [match_mode=exact|subset]
    [zone=<zone>] [table=<table>] [chain=<chain>]
    [direction=<direction>] [action=<action>] [proto=<proto>]
    [sport=<port>] [dport=<port>] [saddr=<cidr>] [daddr=<cidr>]
    [in_iface=<iface>] [out_iface=<iface>] [comment_contains=<text>]
    [limit=<n>] [dry_run=true|false] [require_match=true|false]
    [timeout_ms=<ms>] [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty firewall://.rules.delete rule_id=INPUT:3 dry_run=true

  Shown for a host where iptables is detected, with --json-pretty. rule_id is
  the id rules.delete reports: CHAIN:LINE for iptables (the line within its
  table and chain), the handle for nftables, the rule number for ufw, and
  ZONE:KIND:VALUE for firewalld. iptables rules are deleted by their full rule
  specification. Without rule_id, match_mode=exact (the default) selects rules
  whose proto, ports, addresses and interfaces equal the given ones and that
  have no other match conditions; subset selects every rule the filters fit.
  zone limits a firewalld delete to one zone and is rejected for other
  backends. At least one filter or rule_id is required, and match_mode
  cannot be combined with rule_id. A rule_id found in more than one family or
  table is rejected; add family= or table=.
