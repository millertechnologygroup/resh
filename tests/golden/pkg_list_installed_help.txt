Handle: pkg
Verb: list_installed
Definition: Lists installed packages with their versions, optionally filtered by
  name prefix or substring and paginated.

Usage:
  resh pkg://<alias>.list_installed input=<json>
    <json> keys: manager (required), filter, prefix, include_versions
    (default true; false sets version to null), include_repo,
    include_size, include_install_reason, limit (default 500), offset,
    timeout_ms (default 600000), env

Example:
  resh pkg://system.list_installed 'input={"manager":"apt","prefix":"nginx"}'

Example Output:
