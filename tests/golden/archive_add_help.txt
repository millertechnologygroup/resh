Handle: archive
Verb: add
Definition: Adds files or directories to an existing archive by rewriting it,
  optionally replacing entries that already exist, and reports the changes as
  JSON.

Usage:
  resh archive://.add archive=<path> inputs=<json-array>
    [base_dir=<dir>] [includes=<json-array>] [excludes=<json-array>]
    [overwrite=true|false] [keep_existing_dirs=true|false]
    [follow_symlinks=true|false] [preserve_owner=true|false]
    [preserve_permissions=true|false] [preserve_timestamps=true|false]
    [deterministic=true|false] [backup_suffix=<suffix>]
    [tmp_dir=<dir>] [max_entries=<n>] [max_total_bytes=<n>]
    [format=<archive-format>] [compression=auto|none|gzip|xz|zstd]
    [timeout_ms=<n>] [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty archive://.add archive=logs.tar.gz 'inputs=["db.log"]'
