Handle: cert
Verb: chain.info
Definition: Parses a certificate bundle, finds its leaf certificates, and
  reports every chain from each leaf through the bundle and the trust anchors,
  linking issuers by name and signature; a chain is trusted only when
  rustls-webpki validates it to an anchor.

Usage:
  resh cert://<bundle-path>.chain.info
    [trust=none|system|mount|inline|system+mount|system+inline|mount+inline]
    [trust_paths=<path>,...] [ca_file=<pem>] [trust_pem=<pem>]
    [max_depth=<n>] [max_paths=<n>] [include_raw_subjects=true|false]
    [include_raw_issuers=true|false] [include_cert_refs=true|false]
    [encoding=auto|pem|der] [format=json|text]

Example:
  resh cert://./bundle.pem.chain.info trust_paths=ca.pem format=text

Example Output:
  Target: cert://./bundle.pem
  Encoding: pem
  Trust anchors loaded: 1

  Leaf Candidates:
    [0] CN=web.test

  Chain 0 (length=2)
    Trust Status : trusted
    Reason       : Validated to trust anchor CN=Example CA (mount)
    [0] Leaf (target_bundle)
        Subject : CN=web.test
        Issuer  : CN=Example CA
        Key     : ECDSA P-256
    [1] Root (target_bundle, trust anchor)
        Subject : CN=Example CA
        Issuer  : CN=Example CA
        Key     : ECDSA P-256, CA

  Warnings:
    (none)

  Shown for bundle.pem holding web.pem and ca.pem. trust_status is trusted,
  invalid (ends at an anchor but fails validation), untrusted_root,
  self_signed_untrusted or incomplete. The default trust=none uses only
  trust_paths, ca_file and trust_pem as anchors.
