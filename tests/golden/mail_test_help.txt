Handle: mail
Verb: test
Definition: Checks SMTP settings by opening a real session (TLS and AUTH as
  configured) and closing it, and also sends a test message when
  send_test_email=true.

Usage:
  resh mail://test [smtp_host=<host>] [smtp_port=<n>]
    [use_tls=none|starttls|tls] [smtp_username=<user>] [smtp_password=<pass>]
    [tls_accept_invalid_certs=true|false] [ca_file=<pem>]
    [connection_only=true|false] [send_test_email=true|false]
    [to=<addr,...>] [from=<addr>]
    [subject=<text>] [text_body=<text>] [html_body=<html>]
    [timeout_ms=<ms>] [max_retry=<n>] [retry_backoff_ms=<ms>]
    [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh mail://test connection_only=true
