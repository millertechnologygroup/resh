Handle: archive
Verb: list
Definition: Lists the entries of an archive with their sizes, modes, owners, and
  modification times, as JSON.

Usage:
  resh archive://.list archive=<path> [includes=<json-array>]
    [excludes=<json-array>] [include_metadata=true|false]
    [include_compressed_size=true|false] [max_entries=<n>]
    [max_total_bytes=<n>] [format=<archive-format>]
    [compression=auto|none|gzip|xz|zstd]
    [fail_on_missing_archive=true|false] [timeout_ms=<n>]
    [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty archive://.list archive=logs.tar.gz
