Handle: archive
Verb: test
Definition: Verifies the integrity of an archive by reading every entry and its
  data, reporting a per-entry status and an overall valid flag as JSON.

Usage:
  resh archive://.test archive=<path> [verify_data=true|false]
    [report_entries=true|false] [stop_on_first_error=true|false]
    [max_entries=<n>] [max_total_bytes=<n>] [max_file_bytes=<n>]
    [format=<archive-format>] [compression=auto|none|gzip|xz|zstd]
    [fail_on_missing_archive=true|false] [timeout_ms=<n>]
    [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty archive://.test archive=logs.tar.gz
