Handle: plugin
Verb: remove
Definition: Removes an installed plugin's files from the plugin store,
  optionally purging its data, or only reports the planned steps when dry_run is
  true.

Usage:
  resh plugin://<plugin-id>.remove [force=true|false]
    [purge=true|false] [dry_run=true|false] [timeout_ms=<ms>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty plugin://aws.remove dry_run=true
