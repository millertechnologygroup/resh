Handle: cron
Verb: enable
Definition: Re-enables matching jobs by uncommenting crontab lines or by
  enabling (and by default starting) systemd timers.

Usage:
  resh cron://<label>.enable [id=<id>] [schedule=<expr>]
    [command=<cmd>] [match_command=<text>] [match_comment=<text>]
    [unit_name=<name>] [match_unit=<text>] [backend=both|cron|systemd]
    [scope=all|current|user|system] [users=<user,...>]
    [cron_file=<path>] [unit_scope=auto|user|system|both]
    [start_now=true|false] [require_match=true|false]
    [dry_run=true|false] [format=json|text] [timeout_ms=<ms>]
    every selector given must match; cron_file: /etc/crontab or
    /etc/cron.d/<name> only, and replaces scope

Example:
  resh cron://local.enable match_command=backup backend=cron scope=current
