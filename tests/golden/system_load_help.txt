Handle: system
Verb: load
Definition: Reports the 1, 5, and 15 minute load averages, their values per
  online CPU, and the runnable and total task counts from /proc/loadavg.

Usage:
  resh system://.load [normalize_per_cpu=true|false]
    [include_queue=true|false] [include_human=true|false]
    [include_raw=true|false] [include_paths=true|false]
    [min_cpu_count=<n>] [format=json|text] [input=<json>]
    defaults: normalize_per_cpu, include_queue and include_human true;
      include_raw and include_paths false; min_cpu_count=1 (the
      smallest divisor for per-CPU values); format=json

Example:
  resh --json-pretty system://.load
