Handle: plugin
Verb: available.info
Definition: Reports index details for one plugin selected by name or id,
  optionally narrowed by version, channel, OS, and architecture.

Usage:
  resh plugin://available.info name=<name>|id=<id>
    [version=<x.y.z>] [channel=stable|beta|nightly] [os=<os>]
    [arch=<arch>] [source=default|official|community]
    [offline=true|false] [timeout_ms=<ms>] [ca_file=<pem>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty plugin://available.info name=resh.docker offline=true
