Handle: plugin
Verb: available.list
Definition: Lists plugins from the catalog built into resh or from a catalog
  file, filtered by query, tags, publisher, and platform, with pagination.

Usage:
  resh plugin://available.list [query=<text>] [tags=<tag>,...]
    [publisher=<name>] [platform=<os>-<arch>] [include_prerelease=true|false]
    [limit=<n>] [offset=<n>] [format=summary|full]
    [source=default|file:<path>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty plugin://available.list query=docker limit=1
