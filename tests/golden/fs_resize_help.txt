Handle: fs
Verb: resize
Definition: Grows or shrinks a filesystem, located by mount point or device, to
  an absolute size or by a delta using resize2fs, xfs_growfs, or btrfs.

Usage:
  resh fs://<alias>.resize target=<dir-or-device>
    size=<size> | delta=<+size|-size> [by=auto|mountpoint|device]
    [size_units=auto|bytes|kilobytes|megabytes|gigabytes|terabytes]
    [mode=grow|shrink|auto] [allow_shrink=true|false]
    [min_free_space_percent=<0-100>]
    [manage_underlying_volume=true|false]
    [volume_resize_only=true|false] [filesystem_resize_only=true|false]
    [require_unmounted_for_shrink=true|false] [force=true|false]
    [dry_run=true|false] [timeout_ms=<ms>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty fs://local.resize target=/mnt/data size=2G

  Shown for an ext4 filesystem with --json-pretty. Sizes accept K, M, G
  and T suffixes (powers of 1024); a bare number counts size_units. A size
  equal to the current size returns action "noop". Shrinking needs
  mode=shrink or auto and allow_shrink=true, is refused for xfs, and needs
  an unmounted ext3/ext4 filesystem; xfs and btrfs are resized while
  mounted. force=true passes -f to resize2fs. Defaults: by=auto,
  mode=grow, size_units=auto (bytes), min_free_space_percent=5,
  require_unmounted_for_shrink=true, timeout_ms=600000.
