Handle: archive
Verb: remove
Definition: Removes entries from an existing archive by exact path, glob
  pattern, or directory prefix, rewriting the archive unless dry_run is set.

Usage:
  resh archive://.remove archive=<path> [paths=<json-array>]
    [patterns=<json-array>] [dir_prefixes=<json-array>]
    [dry_run=true|false] [remove_empty_dirs=true|false]
    [backup_suffix=<suffix>] [tmp_dir=<dir>] [max_entries=<n>]
    [max_total_bytes=<n>] [format=<archive-format>]
    [compression=auto|none|gzip|xz|zstd]
    [fail_on_missing_archive=true|false] [timeout_ms=<n>]
    [format_output=json|text]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty archive://.remove archive=logs.tar.gz 'paths=["db.log"]'
