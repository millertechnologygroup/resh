Handle: cron
Verb: list
Definition: Lists crontab entries for the chosen scope as JSON or text, with
  optional filters, sorting, and next-run times (systemd timers are not listed).

Usage:
  resh cron://<label>.list [scope=current|user|system|all]
    [users=<user,...>] [include_system=true|false]
    [include_files=<path,...>] [state=all|enabled|disabled]
    [match_command=<text>] [match_comment=<text>] [match_user=<text>]
    [match_source=<text>] [include_next_run=true|false]
    [now_unix=<seconds>] [timezone=<IANA zone>] [max_entries=<n>]
    [sort_by=none|user|schedule|command|source] [sort_order=asc|desc]
    [include_raw=true|false] [include_paths=true|false]
    [format=json|text] [timeout_ms=<ms>]
    timezone: zone for next-run times of entries without their own
    CRON_TZ (default: the host's local zone); now_unix: reference
    time for next runs, Unix seconds 0-253402300799 (default now);
    include_raw adds the text of each source; timeout_ms: 60000

Example:
  resh cron://local.list
