Handle: cron

Description:
  The cron handle manages scheduled jobs as crontab entries, through the crontab
  command, or as systemd service and timer units, through systemctl. It can
  list, add, remove, enable, and disable jobs on the local host. The host part
  of the URL is only a label and is ignored, such as cron://local.

Available Verbs:
  list      Lists crontab entries for the chosen scope as JSON or text, with
            optional filters, sorting, and next-run times (systemd timers are
            not listed).
  add       Adds a scheduled job as a crontab line or as a systemd service and
            timer pair, preferring systemd when backend is auto.
  enable    Re-enables matching jobs by uncommenting crontab lines or by
            enabling (and by default starting) systemd timers.
  disable   Disables matching jobs without deleting them by commenting out
            crontab lines or by disabling (and by default stopping) systemd
            timers.
  rm        Removes matching jobs from crontabs and deletes matching systemd
            timers, by default along with their unit files.

Usage:
  resh cron://<label>.<verb>[(key=value,...)]

For help with a specific verb:
  resh cron <verb> --help
