Handle: dns
Verb: resolve
Definition: Resolves a host name to addresses after following CNAMEs, or
  resolves mail, service, or reverse records depending on mode.

Usage:
  resh dns://.resolve name=<name> [mode=host|mail|service|reverse]
    [family=any|ipv4|ipv6] [servers=<ip,...>] [port=<n>]
    [use_tcp=true|false] [timeout_ms=<ms>] [retries=<n>]
    [dnssec=true|false] [max_cname_depth=<n>] [follow_srv=true|false]
    [validate_reverse=true|false] [want_raw=true|false]
    [format=json|text]

Example:
  resh dns://.resolve name=example.com family=ipv4
