Handle: fs
Verb: quota_summary
Definition: Aggregates the quota usage and limits of one user, group, or
  project, or of every subject, across the mounted filesystems, listing
  filesystems without quotas separately.

Usage:
  resh fs://<alias>.quota_summary [subject=<name-or-id>]
    [subject_type=auto|user|group|project] [all_subjects=true|false]
    [units=auto|blocks|bytes] [include_space=true|false]
    [include_inodes=true|false] [include_grace=true|false]
    [resolve_uid_gid=true|false]
    [include_mountpoints=<path,...>] [exclude_mountpoints=<path,...>]
    [include_types=<type,...>] [exclude_types=<type,...>]
    [include_sources=<source,...>] [exclude_sources=<source,...>]
    [timeout_ms=<ms>]

Example:
  resh --json-pretty fs://local.quota_summary subject=alice
