Handle: system
Verb: cpu
Definition: Samples /proc/stat twice, sample_duration_ms apart, and reports
  utilization percentages for that interval, with topology, frequency, and
  cgroup CPU limits.

Usage:
  resh system://.cpu [sample_duration_ms=<n>] [sample_min_ms=<n>]
    [per_cpu=true|false] [include_topology=true|false]
    [include_frequency=true|false] [include_cgroup=true|false]
    [include_human=true|false] [include_raw=true|false]
    [include_paths=true|false] [format=json|text] [input=<json>]
    defaults: sample_duration_ms=250 (0 uses sample_min_ms; at most
      60000), sample_min_ms=50, per_cpu, include_topology,
      include_frequency, include_cgroup and include_human true;
      include_raw and include_paths false; format=json

Example:
  resh --json-pretty system://.cpu per_cpu=false
