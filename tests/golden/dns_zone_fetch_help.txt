Handle: dns
Verb: zone.fetch
Definition: Transfers a zone with AXFR, or its changes since a serial with IXFR,
  over TCP from the given or authoritative name servers.

Usage:
  resh dns://.zone.fetch zone=<zone> [servers=<ip,...>] [port=<n>]
    [transfer=AXFR|IXFR] [serial=<n>] [use_tcp=true]
    [timeout_ms=<ms>] [retries=<n>] [dnssec=true|false]
    [max_records=<n>] [prefer_ipv6=true|false]
    [tsig_key_name=<name>] [tsig_secret=<base64>]
    [tsig_algorithm=<alg>] [include_raw=true|false]
    [format=json|text]

Example:
  resh dns://.zone.fetch zone=example.com servers=192.0.2.53
