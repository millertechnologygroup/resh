Handle: log

Description:
  The log handle reads the most recent lines of a plain-text log file or of a
  systemd service's journal, and can follow them as they are written. A file is
  named by the URL path, as in log:///var/log/syslog (absolute) or
  log://./logs/app.log (relative), and log://svc/<service> reads the service's
  journal through journalctl.

Available Verbs:
  tail   Prints the last lines (default 100) of a log file or service journal,
         optionally keeping only those that contain a pattern and following new
         lines.

Usage:
  resh log://{/<path>|./<path>|svc/<service>}.<verb>[(key=value,...)]

For help with a specific verb:
  resh log <verb> --help
