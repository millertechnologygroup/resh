Handle: system
Verb: disk
Definition: Reports capacity, inode usage, and I/O counters for mounted
  filesystems, skipping pseudo filesystems such as proc and tmpfs by default.

Usage:
  resh system://.disk [mount_points=<list>] [devices=<list>]
    [fs_types=<list>] [include_virtual=true|false] [max_mounts=<n>]
    [include_io=true|false] [include_fs_types=true|false]
    [include_human=true|false] [include_raw=true|false]
    [include_paths=true|false] [timeout_ms=<n>] [format=json|text]
    [input=<json>]
    mount_points, devices: each must be mounted (else exit 1)
    defaults: include_virtual=false, max_mounts=64, include_io,
      include_fs_types and include_human true; include_raw and
      include_paths false; timeout_ms=30000; format=json

Example:
  resh --json-pretty system://.disk mount_points=/
